- Customers in France: amounts in euros, stated as TTC or indicatif, with the France context and the date of the price snapshot.
- Formal address: calm, direct, formal « vous ».
- Renters and apartment residents are an explicit context: "Tenant and apartment contexts: removable solutions, private openings only, common areas excluded."
- Compatibility and installation are « à confirmer avec un conseiller », so a human adviser sits between the document and the customer.
